**Cygnus and the Solitary Tree**\
**Image Credit & Copyright:** 2025 Horacio Lander / [AstroHoracio](http://astrohoracio.com/)\
**Text:** [Keighley Rockcliffe](https://kerockcliffe.com/) ([NASA](https://science.gsfc.nasa.gov/sci/bio/keighley.e.rockcliffe) [GSFC](https://www.nasa.gov/goddard/), [UMBC CSST](https://csst.umbc.edu/directory/), [CRESST II](https://cresst2.umd.edu/))

**Explanation:** A lone tree stands in a quiet meadow in [Guadalajara, Spain](https://www.guadalajara.es/en/tourism/discover-guadalajara/), silhouetted against [the Cygnus region](https://obsidian.md/ap210211.html) rising above like flames in the night sky. This deep night skyscape is a composite of exposures that reveals a range of brightness and color human eyes can't quite see on their own. Spanning over a thousand times the [angular size](https://earthsky.org/astronomy-essentials/sky-measurements-degrees-arc-minutes-arc-seconds/) of the full moon, [Cygnus](https://noirlab.edu/public/education/constellations/cygnus/) sets the sky afire with [active star formation](https://courses.lumenlearning.com/towson-astronomy/chapter/star-formation/) where clouds of gas and dust collapse under gravity until [nuclear fusion](https://courses.ems.psu.edu/astro801/content/l5_p4.html) ignites and new stars are born. These stars [ionize](https://energyeducation.ca/encyclopedia/Ionization) the surrounding hydrogen gas, causing it to [glow crimson](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/webb/science-overview/science-explainers/spectroscopy-101-how-absorption-and-emission-spectra-work/), while tendrils of [interstellar dust](https://obsidian.md/ap251119.html) absorb some of that light and cast dark shadows across the sky. Cygnus is a trove of celestial treasures, notably the [Veil](https://obsidian.md/ap250602.html), [Crescent](https://obsidian.md/ap251128.html), and [Pelican](https://obsidian.md/ap210301.html) nebulae, as well as [Cygnus X-1](https://obsidian.md/ap240828.html), the first confirmed [black hole](https://science.nasa.gov/universe/black-holes/). Cygnus continues to yield fresh science, including [a new three-dimensional model](https://chandra.harvard.edu/deadstar/cygnus.html) of the [Cygnus Loop](https://obsidian.md/ap200928.html) made possible by [the Chandra X-ray Observatory](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/chandra-x-ray-observatory/).
