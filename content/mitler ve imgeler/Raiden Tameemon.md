## Raiden Tameemon

Raiden Tameemon is a character in the manga and anime series _Record of Ragnarok_ (_Shūmatsu no Valkyrie_), adapted from the historical Japanese sumo legend of the same name. He represents humanity in the tournament against the gods, known for his unmatched physical strength and mastery of sumo wrestling.

### Key Facts

- **Birth name:** Tarōkichi Seki

- **Role:** Fifth human representative in Ragnarok

- **Opponent:** Shiva

- **Voice actor (Japanese):** Subaru Kimura

- **Affiliation:** Humanity, Einherjar

- **First appearance:** Manga Chapter 31 / Anime Episode 18 

### Background

Raiden’s story reimagines the Edo-period sumo champion as a heroic spirit fighting for humankind’s survival. Born with abnormal muscular power that could crush his own bones, he created “Hundred Seals” to contain his strength. Despite this burden, he became Japan’s greatest rikishi, revered for his compassion and humility. His motivation in Ragnarok is not only to defend humanity but to finally experience an opponent worthy of his full strength.

### Personality

Raiden is portrayed as jovial, passionate, and indulgent—driven by food, sleep, and love—yet grounded in honor and empathy. Though often seen as lustful or carefree, he embodies a protector’s heart, using his power to aid others. In combat, his demeanor shifts to complete seriousness, valuing the purity of sumo above all else .

### Powers and Abilities

Raiden’s power centers on **muscle control**, allowing him to manipulate and redistribute mass across his body for offense or defense. After forging a **Völundr** bond with the valkyrie Thrud, he gains the _Supramuscular Exoskeletal Mawashi_, granting full mastery over his immense strength. His techniques include:

- **Yatagarasu:** A devastating palm strike capable of tearing through divine defenses.

- **Midare Botan:** A limb-crushing grapple that disabled one of Shiva’s arms.

- **Miyama:** Defensive arm expansion forming a living barrier .

### Legacy in the Story

Raiden’s duel with Shiva becomes one of _Record of Ragnarok_’s most emotional bouts. He dies standing, grateful to have unleashed his authentic sumo without restraint.
