## Rokurota

Rokurota, also known as “The Giant of Bizen,” is a minor antagonist in the manga and anime series _Hell's Paradise: Jigokuraku_. He is an eight-year-old child afflicted with gigantism whose immense strength and tragic simplicity make him both terrifying and pitiable. Though outwardly monstrous, his actions stem from a childlike hunger and confusion.

### Key facts

- **Full name:** Rokurōta (陸郎太)

- **Alias:** The Giant of Bizen

- **Age:** 8

- **Height:** 321 cm

- **Race:** Human

- **Status:** Deceased

- **Japanese voice:** Hinata Tadokoro / Kyoko Chikiri (child)

- **English voice:** Ray Hurd / Morgan Lea (child)

### Background and role

Born unusually large, Rokurota accidentally killed his parents and devastated his village through uncontrollable bursts of strength. Labeled a criminal, he was sentenced to death but later chosen for the Shogunate’s expedition to the mystical island of Shinsenkyō in search of the Elixir of Life. On the island, his primal hunger drives him to attack other participants, bringing him into fatal conflict with Gabimaru and Yamada Asaemon Sagiri.

### Appearance and personality

Rokurota is depicted as a masked, towering child with messy dark hair and a muscular build, typically wearing a ragged sleeveless robe. Despite his monstrous frame and violence, his personality retains the innocence of a small boy—he cries from hunger and seeks companionship, unaware of the destruction he causes. His tragedy lies in being a child trapped within a body and power beyond his control.

### Abilities

Rokurota possesses immense physical power, capable of killing opponents with a single blow and tearing trees from the ground. His strength is matched by high speed and durability, enhanced by instinctive use of _Tao_, a mystical energy that hardens his body. Even skilled fighters like Gabimaru struggle to harm him. Ultimately, exhaustion and suffocation from smoke allow Sagiri to end his life mercifully.

### Legacy

Rokurota stands out in _Hell’s Paradise_ as a symbol of innocence corrupted by circumstance. His character’s design and behavior draw inspiration from the mythic giant Daidarabotchi and horror icon Leatherface, blending mythic tragedy with modern horror imagery.
